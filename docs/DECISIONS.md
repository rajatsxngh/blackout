| D063 | 2026-11-07 | Fuel model, where it departs from PRD 8.7 or fills gaps. **Buckets:** 1-minute buckets for the first 30 minutes, then 5-minute buckets to 120 (48 in all). Fuel counts from the first bucket starting at or after the delivery minute. **Objective:** 1000 x w x (240 - bucket start) per dark minute; 1,200 x w per liter short of a 60-minute end reserve (hospitals still off the grid at the end); 120 per minute of driving and per minute until each delivery; 30 minutes' worth for changing a truck's current trip; minus 1 per liter delivered (unloading takes 5 minutes whatever the amount, so deliveries should be as big as fits; without it the model picked arbitrary partial amounts). **Options:** trips only to hospitals off the grid in at least one bucket (fuel elsewhere can't change the objective); a truck already driving a trip can't choose idle; a loading truck must deliver straight from its depot. **Speed:** a complete hint (the previous plan where it still applies, else the greedy plan; fill up, deliver everything aboard), `cp_model_presolve=False` and `linearization_level=2`.
